Trailblazer: health data

Trailblazer is a private training app used only by its owner.

With your permission it reads steps, weight, body fat, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, VO2max and workouts from Health Connect. They are sent only to your own Trailblazer account at gymandtonic.dev (behind Cloudflare Access) to build your training plan, charts and nutrition targets.

Nothing is shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising. Revoke access at any time in Health Connect settings.
